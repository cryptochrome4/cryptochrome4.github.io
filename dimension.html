XRP whales add 72M tokens as ETF assets dip below $1B

TLDR

On-chain analytics show XRP whales added about 72 million tokens in 24 hours, part of more than 452 million accumulated recently, while spot XRP ETF assets have slipped below $1 billion.

Deep dive

A report cited by Tokenpost, drawing on Santiment data, says large XRP holders bought roughly 72 million tokens near the $1 level over the past day. That extends a multi-week trend, with whale wallets adding over 452 million XRP and boosting their combined holdings to around 12.18 billion.

At the same time, SoSoValue data shows spot XRP ETF inflows have slowed, pulling total ETF assets down to about $942 million, under the $1 billion mark. The divergence highlights how whale buying is currently helping support XRP while institutional ETF demand softens.

Conclusion

Key signals to watch are whether whale accumulation around $1 continues and if ETF inflows stabilize or rebound; together they will help indicate whether XRP holds its support zone or faces renewed downside.

XRP bridge incident drains ~200,000 XRP, leaving side undercollateralized

TLDR

Roughly 200,000 XRP were drained across 94 bridge payments, leaving the Coreum side of the XRP bridge undercollateralized while the XRP Ledger itself remained unaffected.

Deep dive

Multiple reports describe an exploit of deposit-detection logic on a cross-chain bridge linking the XRP Ledger to the Coreum/tx ecosystem. According to BSCNews and other X accounts, the attacker minted bridged XRP against deposits that never actually arrived, then swapped those bridged tokens back for real XRP, draining about 200,000 XRP in 94 transactions.

The bridge's relayers correctly signed the transfers based on flawed internal records, and txEcosystem has indicated that bridged XRP on its chain is no longer fully backed. Commentary from community accounts stresses that the vulnerability was in the bridge software, not in the XRP Ledger itself.

Conclusion

Watch for an official post-mortem from the bridge operators, including details on how they will patch the deposit-detection logic, whether they will recapitalize the undercollateralized side, and any plans to compensate affected users before reopening cross-chain transfers.
